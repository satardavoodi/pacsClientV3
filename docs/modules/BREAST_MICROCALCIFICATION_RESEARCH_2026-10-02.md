# Breast microcalcification route selection

Checked: 2026-10-02. Status: research decision; no new clinical training or rollout.

## Decision

Prefer a dedicated native-resolution microcalcification branch, retaining the existing mass detector as a comparator and a separate task. The first controlled experiment should compare adapted DeepMiCa segmentation with a compact two-scale candidate verifier against the same segmentation without the verifier. Retain a multiscale Hessian/DoG plus learned verifier route as an independent fallback. Neither route is established as best by the current evidence.

The immediate priority is label completeness and native signal preservation, not a larger backbone. VinDr-Mammo does not exhaustively mark benign calcifications. Do not train all unboxed tissue as calcification-free. Physician region rectangles are useful weak localization labels, not dense punctum masks. A pixel-annotated dataset is needed for supervised punctum segmentation; verified artifact/background samples are needed for rejection training.

## Task and comparison contract

The requested primary task is autonomous detection of real microcalcifications, with and without a mass, including benign appearances. Malignancy grading, morphology/distribution classification, mass classification and cancer outcome are distinct secondary tasks. Vascular or benign calcifications must not automatically be mislabeled as normal background merely because a published system excludes them. Agree a separate subtype taxonomy for macrocalcifications and vascular appearances.

Compare at individual-punctum, cluster and image/exam levels. Report cluster sensitivity at 0.5, 1, 2 and 4 verified false-positive clusters per image, positive-image miss rate, image-level specificity/NPV, and retained sensitivity after each cascade stage. NPV must include the cohort prevalence and confidence interval. Do not infer NPV from counts of unmarked regions. Penalize oversized clusters and duplicate detections with a frozen matching rule. Measure full-image latency and VRAM, not a single patch forward pass.

Proposed research aspirations are at least 95% cluster sensitivity at no more than one verified false-positive cluster per image, with a 98% sensitivity stretch point reported alongside its actual false-positive burden. These are provisional experiment targets, not achieved results or clinical acceptance criteria. Patient-separated evaluation and independent external data are required; zero misses on a small development sample does not establish universal 100% sensitivity.

## Primary-source findings

| Route | Published evidence | Applicability and reuse status |
|---|---|---|
| [DeepMiCa, 2023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0169260723001499) | Patch-based U-Net segmentation; segmentation AUROC 0.95 and IoU about 0.74; subsequent malignancy AUROC 0.89. | Segmentation and malignancy numbers do not establish full-image FROC. [Author repository](https://github.com/ales-git/DeepMiCa) supplies code and checkpoint links; exact local segmentation checkpoint already executed. Product weight rights and upstream training membership remain unverified. Local label-blind preprocessing differs from the upstream ROI-dependent processing. |
| [Pesapane et al., 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10630180/) | AlexNet reports sensitivity 0.98, specificity 0.89 and AUC 0.98 for presence detection; 1,986 mammograms from 1,000 patients. | Results are patch-level, using 112-pixel crops, and the paper describes patch partitioning. Patient separation is not established by that description. The result supports a compact verifier hypothesis; it is not a full-image screening performance guarantee. Public reusable checkpoint not established. |
| [HDoGReg, 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.00754) | Hessian/DoG candidates followed by CNN regression, on 435 mammograms from two datasets. Mean image IoU 0.670; object TPR 0.744 at 0.4 false detections per square centimetre. | Relevant independent two-stage design. FP/cm2 cannot be equated with FP/image without evaluated breast area. Ready checkpoint/code rights not verified in this survey; estimate implementation effort before allocating a large run. |
| [Multiscale NEO screening CAD, 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6737166/) | Reported maximum cluster sensitivity 100% at 2.59, 0.68 and 1.78 FP/image on DDSM, private PGIMER and INbreast respectively. INbreast comparison has only 26 target clusters. | Candidate enhancement, reduction and clustering support a lightweight comparator. Finite-cohort results are not a guarantee. Published target-cluster definition differs from exhaustive detection of every benign punctum. |
| [Generative refinement, 2025 preprint](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41356355/) | Segmentation output refined by reconstructing calcification-free appearance. Reported pixel PPV rises from 3.2% to 7.3%, retaining sensitivity above 95%. | Pixel PPV is not cluster-box PPV. [Full preprint](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12676427/) describes external INbreast evaluation and institutional negative-region training. Promising artifact-rejection research; low absolute PPV and unverified reusable assets make it a later branch. |
| [MC-GenRef, April 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04470) | Synthetic microcalcifications on real negative backgrounds, then test-time generative posterior refinement; designed for scarce dense labels and domain shift. | Interesting synthetic-supervision fallback. [Official repository](https://github.com/hwcho-research/MC-GenRef) currently exposes a README saying implementation will be available upon publication, not a runnable checkpoint bundle. Do not describe it as ready for immediate reuse. |
| Existing FCOS / YOLOX candidates | Local bounded development screens retained FCOS P3 over tested YOLOX-Tiny and whole-image CLAHE variants. | Short optimization trials do not establish architecture convergence or global superiority. Keep as comparators; avoid further family switching without testing a specific signal, label or sampling hypothesis. |

This is a targeted primary-source review of classical, detector, segmentation, compact classifier and generative routes. It is not a systematic review or proof that every current commercial system has been evaluated. Screening cancer AUC papers without calcification localization evidence cannot establish the requested detection performance.

## Dataset decisions

| Dataset | Verified published information | Recommended role and limitations |
|---|---|---|
| [VinDr-Mammo v1.0.0](https://physionet.org/content/vindr-mammo/1.0.0/) | 5,000 four-view exams. Explicitly excludes BI-RADS 2 finding boxes; only BI-RADS 3–5 findings receive rectangles. Restricted health-data agreement. | Useful digital domain adaptation and suspicious-cluster localization. Unboxed or No Finding samples are not exhaustive all-calcification negatives. Audit local source provenance and retain unknown masks. |
| [CBIS-DDSM](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/cbis-ddsm/) | Separate calcification metadata, ROI masks and pathology labels; scanned-film source, CC BY 3.0. | Existing data remain useful for localization and later malignancy tasks. Inspect each mask's semantics; cluster ROI masks must not be assumed to delineate individual puncta. Split by canonical person, not each derived DICOM PatientID. |
| [Sequential microcalcification dataset, KIOS/Loizidou, 2021](https://zenodo.org/records/5036062) | Publisher describes 100 temporal mammogram pairs, normal/benign/suspicious cases, individual calcification annotations by two radiologists, biopsy results in 32 suspicious cases; archive about 3 GB, attribution license. | Highest-priority new data audit for dense segmentation and verified negative truth. Actual downloaded inventory, mask format, complete annotation scope, patient pairing and license version remain to inspect. Keep prior/recent and CC/MLO views of a person in one split. No subtraction model is required for a single-image baseline. |
| INbreast | Original FFDM data with detailed finding annotations; used by DeepMiCa and HDoGReg. | Valuable masks/geometry when access and exact annotation completeness are established. An upstream INbreast-trained checkpoint cannot be independently qualified on overlapping INbreast cases. Original access/rights must be verified; avoid augmented third-party repackaging as independent patients. |
| [MEXBreast v2, 2025](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/ym4mm9xvhm/2) | 620 unmarked PNG mammograms plus separate marked copies and CSV cluster coordinates; 50/70/100-micron resolutions; CC BY 4.0. | Strong candidate for external cluster localization/domain-shift testing. No advertised dense punctum masks or balanced normal cohort. Inspect person identifiers and whether resolutions represent related images; group all variants. Train on unmarked originals only. |
| [Dryad cascade dataset](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.jm6k3) | Publisher describes 49 mammograms with individual point coordinates and cluster boxes, plus mini-MIAS coordinate references. | Small secondary localization benchmark. Check source/format/license and overlap; do not assume a point annotation is a full segmentation mask. |
| RSNA screening cancer dataset | Outcome classification task rather than exhaustive punctum mask annotation. | Potential later representation pretraining under its terms, not the next direct dense-detection training source. |

New datasets were discovered and publisher pages verified, but no new archive was downloaded or accepted into training during this review.

## Local evidence and its limits

The physician review contains 59 region rectangles in 46 mammographic crops from 23 source images after exclusion of two non-mammographic crops. Overlaps/repeated findings need deduplication. Quality fields remain unrated even though the owner attests completion. At DeepMiCa threshold 0.5, 51 reference regions contain proposal centers and 201 centers lie outside all reference regions; at 0.7 the corresponding counts are 53 and 126. These are development coverage proxies, not sensitivity, confirmed FP counts or NPV. Selected red boxes alone covered 11 regions, demonstrating that proposal selection also needs attention.

Simple native multiscale top-hat at threshold 24 produces 12,927 accepted components in the reviewed crops. It is unsuitable as the final detector in that configuration. It does not disprove a learned multiscale cascade. DeepMiCa inference used peak allocated VRAM 2.196 GiB and 56 seconds for 23 images on the existing A100. Larger GPU hardware is not the demonstrated constraint.

## Controlled next experiments

1. Audit native DICOM against derived PNG: bit depth, photometric inversion, modality/VOI transform, saturation, pixel spacing and punctum contrast. Export original high-bit-depth inputs into protected research storage. User display-window adjustments remain display metadata until a controlled preprocessing ablation is completed. Absolute brightness is not a portable physical density measurement across mammography processing.
2. Audit the KIOS archive and existing data mask semantics. Freeze person-linked train/calibration/test partitions before patch extraction. Preserve already reviewed cases in development; use independent patients for qualification. Remove non-mammographic data, duplicates and burned-in labels from image inputs without altering target findings.
3. Establish a segmentation baseline with native-scale, overlapping 256-pixel tiles; compare with existing non-overlap tiling. Blend tile borders and verify geometry. Use genuine punctum masks for supervised positive pixels and reviewed negative background. Test a recall-weighted loss against the baseline, measuring small-punctum losses and dense-breast errors rather than assuming a particular loss solves imbalance.
4. Add a compact ResNet18-scale verifier with a small native-detail patch and a larger context patch. Use adjudicated hard negatives from actual model errors, not every unboxed VinDr patch. Physician cluster boxes can support weak/region supervision or multiple-instance learning, not fabricated dense masks. Keep representative positive/negative replay; mine training-partition errors only.
5. Compare segmentation alone, segmentation plus verifier, and one independent multiscale candidate plus verifier. Calibrate each threshold separately at matched verified FP/image. Quantify every true finding rejected by the second stage. Stop a branch if its rejection gain sacrifices the predeclared sensitivity floor or quality/geometry cannot be verified.
6. Keep generative refinement and synthetic punctum injection as second-round experiments after simpler candidates. Synthetic findings must reflect acquisition blur, spacing and contrast; never use generated masks as real clinical truth. Do not use super-resolution-generated puncta as evidence of a finding.

Initial budget proposal: A100 jobs capped at 8 GiB each as previously demonstrated, successive single-worker runs, at most four GPU-hours for the first pilot per retained route after data readiness. This is an experiment cap, not an estimate of full convergence. Log actual updates, seeds, source/checkpoint hashes, sensitivity/FP curves, per-stage misses and full-image serving cost. Expand only when a learning curve or measured resource bottleneck justifies it. No H200 requirement has been established.

Promotion requires improvements on common patient-separated evaluation at the requested sensitivity/FP operating point, with subgroup results by breast density, pixel spacing/scanner, subtlety and associated mass. Architectural promotion and clinical release remain separate. All production inference continues through the authenticated Eagle Eye Server.
